## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH (EN) - REFERENCE
### 1. Scope of Service
1.1. This agreement governs the licensing and operations of the B2B SaaS platform provided by Artron LLC (ID: 412799431, Nikea st., N 46A, apt. 17, Kutaisi, Georgia) for sports facilities and federations.
1.2. The services include:
- **SaaS Web Panel:** Web interface for facility managers containing CRM tools, billing, trainers commission splits, and operations analytics.
- **IoT Turnstile Relay API:** Hardware controller integration API operating door latches and turnstiles in real-time via Socket/TCP protocols.
- **Athlete Mobile Integration:** Client-facing mobile application facilitating dynamic QR entrance passes, session bookings, and online membership purchases.

### 2. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & 99.9% Uptime Guarantee
2.1. The Provider guarantees 99.9% monthly server and API endpoint availability (Uptime Guarantee).
2.2. Uptime is computed on a monthly cycle. If the SLA target is breached, the Tenant is eligible to receive service credits proportional to the downtime.
2.3. The guarantee excludes downtime due to force majeure, ISP routing errors, and local utility outages at the Tenant's physical venues.

### 3. Pricing Tiers & Subscription Billing
3.1. Fees are charged based on the selected tier (Starter, Pro, Enterprise).
3.2. Subscriptions are billed on a recurring monthly or annual pre-paid cycle, with payments due within 5 calendar days of the billing start date.
3.3. Failure to settle outstanding fees grants the Provider the right to suspend Web Panel access and turnstile relay synchronization.

### 4. Compliance with Georgian Labor Inspection Order №01-15/ნ
4.1. The integrated work-time tracking system is strictly built to comply with Order №01-15/n of the Minister of Labour, Health and Social Affairs of Georgia.
4.2. Physical check-in/out logs automatically populate electronic timesheets for Labor Inspection compliance checks. The Tenant holds final liability for timesheet correctness.

### 5. Data Processing Agreement (DPA) & AES-256-GCM Encryption
5.1. For all uploaded athlete and staff records, the Provider acts as the Technical Data Processor, while the Tenant acts as the Data Controller.
5.2. Both parties shall adhere to the Georgian Personal Data Protection Law and GDPR.
5.3. Sensitive personal identifiers (National IDs) are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM column-level database encryption.
5.4. Biometric profiles (facial entry validation photos) and medical clearances are strictly governed under Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

### 6. Termination, 30-Day Offboarding Export & 3-Year Archival
6.1. Upon subscription termination, Tenant's operational datasets are preserved for 30 calendar days to enable offboarding data export.
6.2. After the 30-day offboarding period, all operational records are permanently purged from live databases.
6.3. In compliance with the Tax Code of Georgia, financial transaction ledgers and purchase logs are archived for 3 years before final deletion.
